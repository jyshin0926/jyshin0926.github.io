## Summary
M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence at Seoul National University’s Music and Audio Research Group, advised by Professor Kyogu Lee. ## Research Projects
- **D-UNIQE: Descriptive Unified Speech Quality and Emotion Assessment**, 2026. First author; under review at ICASSP 2027. A unified evaluation model for speech quality and emotional expressiveness in emotional TTS, producing descriptive judgments grounded in human perception. [Listening demo](https://jyshin0926.github.io/DUNIQE/).
- **SEAM: Speech Emotion-Aligned Controllable Music Generation**, 2026. First author; under review at ICASSP 2027. A controllable music generation system conditioned on emotion extracted from speech. [Listening demo](https://jyshin0926.github.io/SEAM/).
- **Emergent Vocal Communication: Multi-Agent Referential Game**, 2026–Present. Industry-sponsored project with Nongshim at SNU MARG, studying communication between agents with physically modeled vocal tracts and auditory systems. Contributing to dialogue protocols and evaluation of emergent communication.
