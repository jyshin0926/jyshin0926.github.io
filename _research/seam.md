---
title: "SEAM: Speech Emotion-Aligned Controllable Music Generation"
excerpt: "Speech-conditioned music generation with controllable emotional alignment."
collection: research
---

SEAM explores how speech can guide the emotional character of generated music. The demo compares generated audio with baseline methods across angry, happy, sad, and excited speech, and includes an intensity-steering task. This first-author work is under review at ICASSP 2027.
